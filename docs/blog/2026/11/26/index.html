Part I. Illumina BioInsight: Product portfolio overview

Illumina BioInsight is Illumina’s business division focusing on biological data, bioinformatics software, data analytics and artificial intelligence (AI). Its objective is to provide an end-to-end informatics ecosystem that transforms sequencing data into biological and clinical insights.

Illumina launched its unified BioInsight Platform in October 2026, bringing together its previously separate informatics products under a common software environment, account and subscription model (launch announcement).

The important distinction is that these products are not all designed for the same type or stage of analysis. Some process raw sequencing data, some perform downstream biological analysis, some interpret genetic variants, and others provide the underlying computational infrastructure.

3. What does each product actually do?

3.1 BaseSpace Sequence Hub (BSSH)

Sequencing data management and analysis portal

BaseSpace is primarily the interface connecting sequencing instruments, sequencing data and ready-to-use bioinformatics applications.

  • Monitor sequencing runs remotely.
  • Review sequencing QC metrics, such as Q30 and yield.
  • Store, download and share sequencing data.
  • Launch preconfigured bioinformatics applications.
  • Manage sequencing projects and analysis results.

3.2 DRAGEN (Dynamic Read Analysis for GENomics)

High-performance secondary analysis engine

DRAGEN is the computational engine that processes sequencing reads and converts them into meaningful molecular measurements. It supports applications including:

  • Whole-genome and whole-exome sequencing.
  • Germline and somatic variant calling.
  • RNA-seq alignment and gene quantification.
  • Structural variant and copy number variant detection.
  • Methylation, single-cell and spatial analysis for supported assays.

Simple understanding: DRAGEN performs functions comparable to combinations of BWA, STAR, GATK, featureCounts and other secondary analysis tools, depending on the selected workflow.

3.3 Illumina Connected Multiomics (ICM)

Downstream biological discovery and multiomics analysis

This is the product most relevant to researchers performing transcriptomics, single-cell and spatial data analysis.

Unlike DRAGEN, which primarily generates molecular measurements, Connected Multiomics focuses on identifying biological patterns, comparing experimental groups and interpreting results.

3.4 Emedgene

AI-assisted germline variant interpretation

Emedgene focuses on identifying potentially disease-causing inherited genetic variants, particularly for rare genetic disease research.

Part II. Illumina StrataMap Spatial Transcriptome

StrataMap is Illumina’s own sequencing-based spatial transcriptomics technology, officially launched on June 8, 2026. It was previously known as the Illumina Spatial Solution (launch announcement).

It is a direct competitor to 10x Genomics Visium HD, enabling researchers to measure genome-wide gene expression while preserving the spatial location of transcripts within tissue sections.

Unlike Connected Multiomics, StrataMap is not simply bioinformatics software. It is an end-to-end solution covering tissue preparation, spatial RNA capture, library preparation, sequencing and bioinformatics analysis.

5. Potential benefits for CPOS

Considering the existing NovaSeq X Plus infrastructure and spatial transcriptomics analysis services, StrataMap is worth investigating for several reasons:

  • Lower instrument investment: No dedicated spatial instrument is required beyond compatible existing equipment.
  • Larger tissue coverage: Suitable for large tissue sections and multiple tissue samples on one slide.
  • Non-model organisms: Poly(A)-based capture avoids dependence on species-specific probe panels.
  • Integrated bioinformatics: Illumina provides both secondary and tertiary analysis software.
  • Potential new core service: A complementary spatial transcriptomics service alongside Visium HD.

Before introducing the service, evaluate sequencing requirements, cost per sample, DRAGEN output formats, compatibility with Seurat and Scanpy, cloud computing costs, and sensitivity at equivalent sequencing depth.

This assessment is based on published product specifications, not independent experimental benchmarking.

### 3.4 Emedgene

*AI-assisted germline variant interpretation*

Emedgene focuses on identifying potentially disease-causing inherited genetic variants, particularly for rare genetic disease research.

Its functionality includes variant annotation, phenotype-based prioritization, inheritance analysis, pathogenicity evidence assessment and report preparation.

# Part II. Illumina StrataMap Spatial Transcriptome

**StrataMap is Illumina's own sequencing-based spatial transcriptomics technology**, officially launched on June 8, 2026. It was previously known as the Illumina Spatial Solution ([launch announcement](https://www.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2026/582e652d-8a03-475c-87da-db84a18b0b1a.html)).

One important distinction: **1 µm capture resolution does not automatically mean better single-cell gene expression sensitivity.** Actual biological resolution also depends on RNA diffusion, capture efficiency, sequencing depth, binning and cell segmentation. Independent benchmarking would be needed to establish superiority.

## 5. Potential benefits for CPOS

Considering the existing NovaSeq X Plus infrastructure and spatial transcriptomics analysis services, StrataMap is worth investigating for several reasons:

- **Lower instrument investment:** No dedicated spatial instrument is required beyond compatible existing equipment.
- **Larger tissue coverage:** Suitable for large tissue sections and multiple tissue samples on one slide.
- **Non-model organisms:** Poly(A)-based capture avoids dependence on species-specific probe panels.
- **Integrated bioinformatics:** Illumina provides both secondary and tertiary analysis software.
- **Potential new core service:** A complementary spatial transcriptomics service alongside Visium HD.

Before introducing the service, evaluate sequencing requirements, cost per sample, DRAGEN output formats, compatibility with Seurat and Scanpy, cloud computing costs, and sensitivity at equivalent sequencing depth.

This assessment is based on published product specifications, not independent experimental benchmarking.
